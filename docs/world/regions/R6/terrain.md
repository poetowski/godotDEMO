## The south bank

The river's far side, south of the bridge, on the author's paint. The block `R6SouthBank` under `Sectors`
in `field.tscn` owns C to G, rows 17 to 20, from `src/world/field/sectors/r6_south_bank/`. C20, D20, E20,
F20 and G20 came to it from `RiverSouthA` and `RiverSouthF`.

- **Poplars:** tall poplars and thick hazel, dog rose, elder and bramble over F19 and F20.
- **White poplars:** along C20 and D20, with foxgloves and cow parsley.
- **The tent:** a medieval army's pavilion in D19 facing the road, with banners either side of its door,
  a weapon rack, a campfire and stores.
- **The ring:** a ring of stones round an old carved post in E18.
- **The rest:** rocks on stony paint, beaver stumps, fungus logs, shed antlers and small stands of trees.

Trees and plants are worked out at each bake; the rest are props to move by hand. **Placeholder:** all
of the layout.

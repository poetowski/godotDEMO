## Mood

A quiet, green, slightly overgrown waterside: lived-in by plants, not by people.

## Colours

All in `tools/blender/riverside.py`.

- Leaves: deep (0.21, 0.37, 0.17), mid (0.30, 0.47, 0.20), light (0.43, 0.57, 0.25),
  drying (0.63, 0.60, 0.34), close to the field's grass.
- Stone: warm light (0.58, 0.57, 0.52), mid (0.47, 0.48, 0.46), dark (0.37, 0.39, 0.39),
  with moss (0.34, 0.45, 0.20).
- Wood: bleached driftwood (0.63, 0.58, 0.49), wet (0.31, 0.25, 0.19), bark (0.37, 0.31, 0.23).
- Small accents only: white and pink lilies, violet iris, yellow marigold, brown
  cattail heads.

## Ground and materials

Low-poly and flat-shaded, colour from materials, no textures.

## Plants

Water lilies on still water; reeds and cattails at the waterline; sedge, ferns,
blue flag iris and marsh marigold on the banks; willow shrubs and a few young
weeping willows above them.

## Buildings and objects

No buildings. Mossy boulders, river stones, pebbles, driftwood and old stumps.

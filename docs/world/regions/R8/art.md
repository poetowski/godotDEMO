# R8: art

Where this region differs from `docs/world/art.md`.

## Mood

An old forest on the hill between the farm and the drop, darker and quieter than the farm's
trees, with a camp nobody has quite finished leaving.

## Colours

The woodland family's bark and greens, a shade darker. Spruces close to black-green against the
pale cloud of ash crowns. **Placeholder.**

## Ground and materials

The author's painted way, with `grass_grid.gdshader`'s surfaces. Under the trees, leaf litter,
bracken, moss and twigs on the grass.

## Plants

All in `tools/blender/wildwood.py`: spruces of about 15 m and 22 m, young firs, hornbeams, tall
ashes and dead pines, with R1's tall pines and beeches among them.

## Buildings and objects

The camp: a stone-ringed fire burnt down to embers, a tripod and pot, log seats, a patched canvas
tent, one that's come down, a bedroll, a drying rack, crates, a barrel and firewood.

## Light and weather

The campfire's embers light only at night, like the lanterns.
